The Atelier

Made by hand, made but once

Maison Miette is a small couture house dressing 11½″ fashion dolls in garments cut, sewn and finished entirely by hand. We do not run editions or restocks — every gown, set and dress is conceived as a single piece. The satin sash, the tiered tulle, the lace peplum: each is its own story, sewn once and never again.
